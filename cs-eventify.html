Project overview

Eventify is a mobile application designed to help users discover, explore and save events based on their interests. The app brings different types of events into one place, making it easier for users to find new experiences and plan their free time.

Problem

Events are scattered across different platforms Users spend too much time searching for events It can be difficult to find events based on personal interests Important event information is not always easy to find Users may miss events they would be interested in.

Pain points

  • No centralized place for discovering events
  • Too many platforms to search through
  • Difficult to compare different events
  • Hard to keep track of saved events
  • Users don't always receive personalized recommendations.
