Project overview

Planify is a mobile application designed to help users organize their daily routines, build healthy habits and stay consistent with their goals. The app combines task management, habit tracking and daily planning into one simple and motivating experience.

Problem

People often struggle to stay organized and consistent with their daily routines. Using different apps or traditional notes for tasks, habits and goals can make planning feel complicated and overwhelming. Planify was created to provide users with one simple space where they can plan their day, track their habits and monitor their progress.

Pain points

  • Difficult to stay consistent with habits
  • Forgetting daily tasks and routines
  • Lack of motivation to maintain habits
  • Planning feels overwhelming
  • Tasks and habits are often managed separately
  • Difficult to track long-term progress
  • Users don't always know how well they are progressing
