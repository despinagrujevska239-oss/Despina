Project overview

ServiceHub is a mobile application that helps users quickly discover, compare and book local services in one place. The app makes it easier to find trusted professionals, explore available services and manage bookings.

Problem

Finding a reliable service provider can be time-consuming and frustrating. Users often need to search through social media, websites and recommendations before finding the right professional. ServiceHub brings this process together in one simple platform.

Pain points

  • Difficult to find reliable professionals
  • Information is scattered across different platforms
  • Lack of clear service details
  • Difficult to compare options
  • Time-consuming communication and booking
  • Unclear availability and pricing
